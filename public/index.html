Welcome to the wild

More than a safari. A connection with nature.

Far from the noise of the city, Aaramtola–Bhimodi offers a refreshing forest experience. Travel along a scenic jungle trail, listen to birdsong, discover local biodiversity and enjoy the peaceful beauty of Bhimodi Talab.

Our trained safari guides help visitors appreciate the forest, its trees, wildlife and natural heritage. Every journey is about experiencing the whole forest — not just searching for a tiger.
